Featured · Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Sep 2026

ShilpSetu

An AI "virtual business manager" for artisans. A craftsperson photographs a product and describes it by voice; the app returns a Hindi + English catalogue listing, a suggested price and a public shop page with a QR code.

My role: Backend Developer, Team CodeKarigar

  • Built the Python/FastAPI backend with 11 REST API endpoints for accounts, products, AI catalogue, pricing and storefronts.
  • Designed a 6-table relational database with SQLAlchemy (users, businesses, products, pricing, market data, chat history).
  • Secured logins with JWT tokens and bcrypt password hashing.
  • Added an AI fallback chain so catalogue writing and the business-advice chatbot keep working when an AI provider fails.
